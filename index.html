A diagnosis as a loop that fails to settle — the first worked entry of the clinical cache. Cross-referenced to A537.jsx; the cache is a clinical projection of the same pentad.

RESTLESS → SETTLE-BY-MOVING → PACE → COLLIDE → PERSIST → RESTLESS′
A diagnosis is not a list of abnormalities. It is a failed transition: the action is taken and the adjective survives.

The operators

I · Adjective restless — the Subject. Inner agitation in the patient's own words, felt before anything is visible. II · Objective settle by moving — what is represented as the case: the target and repertoire. The felt state acquires a model of its own resolution. III · Errative pace — the representation fails and searches; mismatch becomes motion. Pacing, rocking and shifting are the error made visible (scorable, e.g. Barnes scale). Relief profile is the discriminator. IV · Interactive collide — trajectories meet and interfere: drug × transmitters (D2 · ACh · 5-HT₂A · NA · GABA · Glu) × context × the people around. Set by this patient's drugs, doses and timing. Graded below. V · Survive persist — what gets carried forward: the unresolved urge, sleep lost, distress accumulated. Not a truth or a best choice, only what persists. I′ · Adjective the survivor returns as a new quality — the movement is felt and the adjective is still there. Loop closure: sim(I′, I) ≈ 1 means unresolved.

Grammar of the pentad

I → II Quality to thing: the felt state is given a represented target. II → III Error becomes generative, not merely defective: the Objective proves insufficient, so the system departs from it. III is the errant operator, not a classifier. III → IV Error becomes interaction: unresolved motion collides with drugs, transmitters, environment and other people. This is off-script, not optimisation. IV → V Interaction becomes consequential: something survives. V → I′ Survival returns as a new quality or state.

Revision v0.2. v0.1 read II as intention and put PACE at V (RESTLESS → MOVE → FAIL → CROSS-TALK → PACE → RESTLESS′). Pacing moves to III because it is the errative motion itself and is scored there; V is now the carried-forward state, which is what the agent logs from day to day.

Errative as discriminator — what movement does to the adjective

Akathisia Little or no relief; the urge returns. Tied in time to a drug start, dose change, switch or withdrawal. Restless legs Relief while moving; worse at rest and in the evening; mostly legs. Anxiety Movement or exercise often helps; the driver is threat appraisal, not an urge to move.

Evidence grade on IV

Established Strong association with dopamine-blocking antipsychotics, and also SSRIs/SNRIs and antiemetics such as metoclopramide. Reducing or stopping the culprit (by the prescriber) is first-line; propranolol and low-dose mirtazapine have small-trial support. Contested D2 → striatal ACh as the primary mechanism fits drug-induced parkinsonism better than akathisia: anticholinergics are unreliable here, and SSRIs cause it without D2 blockade. Mesocortical dopamine, 5-HT₂A, noradrenergic overdrive and GABA loss in withdrawal are all live hypotheses. Not computed “Cartesian / Jacobian / Hessian” in the seed thread was metaphor. The real version is per-patient receptor occupancy from published Ki values and drug concentration. Cache IV claims as hypotheses until then.

Two clocks — same pentad, two scales

Inner loop restless → pace → restless. Seconds to minutes. This is where the patient lives. Outer loop drug started or changed → akathisia → recognised → prescriber acts → resolves or persists. Days to weeks. This is where family and agent can act. A537's Darwinian lens (FirstAid / Minutes → OutPatient / Months) supplies the time-bins.

Who sees which operator

Patient I and I′ — the adjective, in their own words. Family / kin III and V — the pacing (searching made visible) and what carries forward: sleeplessness, irritability, worsening across days. Without I, these are misread as behaviour or anxiety. The agent's job is to translate III and V back into I. Clinician III, IV, V plus a rating scale. Family is not the patient: shared risk, shared information, diverging interests (A537, Sovereign Patient / kin layer).

Action boundary — the column outside the pentad

Restlessness that worsens after a medication start, dose change or switch is flagged for same-day prescriber contact. Any thoughts of self-harm are escalated as urgent.

The agent may describe, log and flag. It never advises a dose change or stopping a drug — abrupt benzodiazepine cessation is itself dangerous.

This is A537's Reserve Floor, R(a, xt) ≥ ρ, applied at the bedside, with WATCH as the cross-check.
